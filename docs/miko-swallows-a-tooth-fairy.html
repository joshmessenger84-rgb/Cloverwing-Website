Miko Swallows a Tooth Fairy

Miko’s wobbly tooth fell out in the night… and she SWALLOWED it!

So Luna, a brand-new tooth fairy on her third night ever, has to pop in and find it. Down she goes — GULP! — straight into Miko’s tummy soup.

Can the Emergency Rescue Squad get her out? It’s going to take a feather, two umbrellas and the biggest, bogiest sneeze in the world.

A gloriously snotty tooth fairy adventure for anyone with a wobbly tooth.
