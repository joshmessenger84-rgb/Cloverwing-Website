About the studio

The shop is wherever you are.

Cloverwing Studio is a small publisher of children’s books. We do not have a shop on a real street, so we painted ourselves into a few of our favourite ones.

Our books are printed one at a time, when you order them. Nothing sits in a warehouse, and nothing is sold on this website.

  1. Choose a book from the shelves.
  2. Follow its button to Lulu or Amazon and order there.
  3. Your copy is printed and posted straight to you.
