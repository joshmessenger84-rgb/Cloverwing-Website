Hunters

Observe the strategy. Understand the survival.

Speed, stealth, strength and cooperation: wild hunters have evolved remarkable ways to survive.

Watch, Wonder & Write: Hunters pairs 20 wildlife documentaries with questions that invite you to look closely at animal behaviour, adaptations and life in the wild.

Follow the timestamps, examine what the films reveal and explain your observations in your own words.
